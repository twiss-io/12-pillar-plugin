## The flywheel as a whole

### One system

Life is one system. Business, money, health and relationships are not separate departments with separate tools. They are one person's life, and they need one operating system that holds the whole life, not two (a work system and a personal system). When a life is reset or reviewed, it is reorganised across business, money, health and relationships in a single pass.

Life has twelve pillars, and the framework can be used by anybody. The pillars are not a list. They are a flywheel.

### The chain of currencies

Each pillar gives the next one its currency. Faith gives direction, identity gives clarity, time gives allocation, health gives capacity, development gives skill, relationship gives access, career gives credibility. Branding gives business opportunity. Opportunity becomes finance. Finance becomes lifestyle. Legacy tells you what the outcome is; the outcome you are fighting for is your life vision. Legacy is the summary, and then you go back to your faith.

| # | Pillar | Gives the next pillar |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Faith & Inner Compass | **Direction** |
| 2 | Identity & Life Vision | **Clarity** |
| 3 | Time & Energy | **Allocation** |
| 4 | Health & Longevity | **Capacity** |
| 5 | Personal Development | **Skill** |
| 6 | Relationships | **Access** |
| 7 | Career & Track Record | **Credibility** |
| 8 | Personal Brand & Authority | **Opportunity** |
| 9 | Work, Business & Opportunities | becomes **Finance** |
| 10 | Finance & Wealth | becomes **Lifestyle** |
| 11 | Lifestyle & Experiences | feeds **Legacy** |
| 12 | Legacy & Stewardship | loops back to **Faith** |

### Three consequences of the flywheel

1. **Start anywhere, and momentum spreads.** The system can instantly start governing whichever area most needs a fix, then slowly move to the other areas, because it is a flywheel.
2. **It runs like clockwork.** Everything flows through the flywheel and knows how to get back to where it started. Every pillar knows where it hands off, and the loop returns to its start.
3. **One input feeds many pillars.** Each experience is logged once and auto-tagged (up to ten tags) so that it lands in skill set, career and branding at the same time. The pillars are lenses on one life, not separate filing cabinets.

### The Four P spine: Purpose → Perception → Position → Plan

The core sequence running through the pillars:

- **Purpose** sits in Faith and Identity. With no goal, a person is like a taxi driver: people tell him the destination and he just drives there.
- **Perception** sits in Identity and Brand: how people form the perception about you.
- **Position** sits in Career: based on perception, people give you a position.
- **Plan** sits in Time and Opportunities: you reverse-engineer the plan.

### AIOO: Actions × Inputs → Outputs → Outcome

- Identical actions give different results because inputs differ (resources, family, context, network). Never copy someone's actions 100%; your inputs are different.
- A series of outputs adds up to an outcome.
- Plan backwards: what is the goal? What is the action? What input do you need to put in? What is the output? How do you get the outcome? After you get the result, you re-edit, you change, you try.
- Most of the time, people can't act because they have no purpose. So the key is purpose: understand what our identity is. Who are we? Then what is our goal? This is why the flywheel starts at Faith and Identity.

## 1 · Faith & Inner Compass

**This pillar is** what a person believes and what their faith is, plus the reflective practices around it: meditation, personal review, all of it. It also covers purpose: what is my faith, what do I believe in, what is my purpose.

**What it gives:** **Direction.** Every pillar after it spends time, energy, skill or money, and without a direction that spending has no destination. A person with no inner compass is permanently driving other people to their destinations, like a taxi driver. Faith answers "why", so it sits first. It is also where the loop returns: Legacy goes back to Faith.

### How it works

#### Base belief vs calculation tools (metaphysics as an instrument, never an anchor)

- Metaphysics (BaZi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Thai astrology, numerology and the like) is not used as an anchor or a pillar. It is used as a tool to calculate.
- What the instruments are for: an added layer to strategy, timing, and what to look out for. Primarily two things, risk management and timing control, plus expectation management.
- Expectation management is a counterweight to a known bias. A very positive, aggressive, bullish person needs something to temper them.
- The failure mode: if the chart is used as an anchor or a dependency, it will keep holding onto hope for you. Agency is handed to a chart.
- The method: keep a **base belief** ("my base religion is this; this is my base belief") separate from the instruments consulted. Use the instruments to counterweight your own known bias.

#### Faith gets a fixed budget line

In the 168-hour budget, God is the first relationship allocated: six hours a week (church every week, and care group on alternate weeks). Faith is not left for spare time. It is reserved before work gets anything.

#### Life is a game you play, not a movie you act in

- There is no fixed fate (命数). A person can change it.
- Heaven didn't make you a movie to play a fixed role. It's like GTA: Heaven created a game.
- The metaphor upgrades to SimCity (you build systems) and then to an MMO (other real players, quests).
- The practical core is agency. You are a player making build choices.

#### The personal review, run as a life reset

The review practice of this pillar is the life reset. The instruction to the AI: rebuild my life and context from scratch. Treat this as a new master context. All information may be outdated unless I re-confirm. Help me reorganise my life across business, money, health, relationship.

What makes it work is radical honesty:
- You cannot lie to yourself. There's no way to frame it, reframe it.
- You can make it anything you want, but facts are facts. And how you feel is how you feel.
- A reset can be finished in half an hour by someone who thinks about these questions every day and has thought about them for years. The speed comes from years of review, not from the prompt.

#### How it connects

- **Faith is a governor on the whole system.** The inner layer slows the engine down (expectation management; not bashing the body). The compass sets direction, and it also limits how hard the engine is allowed to run.
- **Honesty is the input quality bar for everything downstream.** If the review is cosmetic, Identity is built on a fiction, the 168 budget protects the wrong things, and the AI coaching the person is a mad AI.
- **Faith is where the loop returns.** Looking at the outcome of a life refreshes the belief about what it's for, and the next turn of the flywheel starts there.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Can say in one sentence why the current work is being done | Busy, reactive, decisions made by default or by whoever asks loudest |
| Reflection has a fixed time slot that survives busy weeks | Reflection only happens when something breaks |
| Instruments (charts, tests, horoscopes) inform timing and risk only | A tool is consulted to say who you are or whether to act |
| The review is honest enough to be uncomfortable | The review reads like a LinkedIn post |

### Questions to ask

- What do you believe your life is for, in one sentence you'd still sign in ten years?
- When did you last do a review you couldn't "frame or reframe"?
- Which way does your natural bias lean, and what tempers it?
- Are you driving someone else's taxi right now? Whose destination is this?

## 2 · Identity & Life Vision

**This pillar is** two halves. **Personal identity:** the Four P (Purpose → Perception → Position → Plan) to help a person understand themselves. **Life vision:** put down lifelong goals, then move towards them; take the life vision and turn it into a vision board.

**What it gives:** **Clarity.** A concrete picture of who the person is and the life they are building. Time cannot allocate hours until clarity says what the hours are for. Clarity is the filter for yes and no.

### How it works

#### History → North Star → Current Season

Write out the history: that's the roots. The future north star: that's the goal. Then there's the current: one season.

- **History (roots):** who am I? Childhood, family composition, schools, education. How I got into business, who my partners are. Because of this opportunity, I learned this; that's where my skills come from. Why ventures were left, years of service, how one thought then. It is deliberately private: it will never be known knowledge and cannot be publicised. Its purpose: so the system understands why the person thinks this way.
- **North Star:** the declarations (below).
- **Current Season:** the stretch between roots and north star. It is temporary by definition.
- **Why it matters:** once all three exist, the train of thought behind any reset can be seen. Hardly anyone has that.
- **Build order:** build the empty framework first, then pour the story in. First turn it into the framework, then put the life story in.

#### Declaration-style goals: write the state of being, not the task

Goals are not put up as tasks. They are written in a declaration style, as a state of being.

- **Present tense, as already true:** "I am extremely healthy, all my major organs and limbs are strong and fully functional. I'm a very good swimmer."
- **Describe the end state and leave the method open:** "I have great eyesight; I don't require visual aid" (LASIK or no LASIK, whatever). "My teeth alignment is perfect", instead of "I'm going to do Invisalign". "I'm free from addiction", instead of "I'm going to quit smoking".
- **Cover every area of life:** health, net worth, marriage, family, friends, business authority, material life, ministry and philanthropy. The declarations map onto all of the pillars.
- **Watch the narrative ethics of the wording:** "I have the best talents working for the corporation, and they are extremely happy and motivated", not "the best talents working for me". That's not the narrative.
- **It reads as a story.** It tells a story about your future. It is not a wish list to show off; sharing goals that way is too much and not palatable.
- **Ritual:** read it every single day.
- **Use them as anchors for diagnosis.** These are hard anchors, so you can tell how you are moving. Some show signs of happening; there are some glaring ones that can be fixed.
- **Why it works:** most of the things written down succeed, because you move yourself towards them. This is how a life is crafted.

#### Self-awareness instruments (the onboarding stack)

- At the personal identity phase, do self-awareness. Use tools like Myers-Briggs, then Enneagram, then DISC, with constant reassessment.
- Onboarding order: put in the BaZi (so the system understands you), then the Enneagram (so you understand yourself), then the vision board (what is your ultimate life north star; if you don't know, just give whatever first), then your life questions, the current situation, then pillar by pillar, wherever you feel you can start.
- Instruments come first because once the Enneagram is in, it's easier to onboard. They give the AI fixed dimensions to hang open-ended answers on.

#### Perception: the outside half of identity (Four E's + three multipliers)

After purpose, you need to understand people's perception of you. People introduce themselves by anchors (their school, their club, their sailing team). Perception is formed by:

- **Education:** what you studied.
- **Exposure:** things you were near and learned to do without owning the result (e.g. an internship).
- **Experience:** areas you've worked in for years *and were responsible for*.
- **Evidence:** your track record; evidence that you are successful in certain things.
- plus **Association** (whose child you are, who you know), **Access** (where you can bring people into), and **Resources** (what you have that others don't and can contribute).

Once a higher perception can be displayed, a person can keep remodelling themselves. Identity can be edited.

#### How it connects

- **Declarations turn vague wishes into a backlog that can be diagnosed.** Because each one describes an end state, the gap between declaration and reality becomes a list of fixes, not a verdict. Health declarations read back become "do the biohack program, do the teeth, do the eyes; everything can be done". That is how Identity hands work to Health, Finance and the rest.
- **History explains the inputs.** In AIOO terms, history is the inputs: family, money, context, network. It tells you, and your AI, which actions will actually work for you.
- **The current season links Identity to Time.** Naming the season is what makes a season of war legitimate.
- **Perception links Identity to Brand and Career.** The Four E's are both an identity audit and the raw material of brand and position.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| The life being built can be described in present tense, concretely | Goals are vague, or are a list of tasks |
| Declarations "showing signs of happening" can be pointed to, and the fix for the glaring gaps named | Goals are never re-read after writing them |
| History is known well enough to explain why one thinks the way one does | Constant comparison; adopting other people's goals |
| How others perceive the person is known, and it matches what is wanted | Perception is accidental, or anchored on something outgrown |

### Questions to ask

- Describe the life you're building, in the present tense. Which sentence are you embarrassed to write, and why?
- Which declarations show signs of happening, and which are glaring gaps with a known fix you're avoiding?
- What part of your history explains a belief or habit that's now holding you back?
- If a stranger met you today, which of the Four E's (and association, access, resources) would they notice first?
- What season are you in right now, and when does it end?

## 3 · Time & Energy

**This pillar is** Time and Energy Mastery: where direction and clarity become a plan for the actual hours of a week. Energy belongs here too; this is where energy levels are described.

**What it gives:** **Allocation.** A deliberate, budgeted split of a fixed resource. Without it, direction and clarity never reach the calendar, and work expands into whatever isn't protected.

### How it works

#### The planning cascade: Year → Quarter → Week

- **Year:** print out 12 pages, January to the end. Write down all the key dates: birthday celebrations, holidays, the pre-planned things. These are the annual commitments.
- **Quarter:** in the next 90 days, what is there? The slots you can't run away from. Pre-plan them. Then see how many slots are left.
- **Week:** a four-week breakdown of the monthly budget, with one hard rule: **this week gone doesn't come back next week.** Time is a budget that expires each week, not a balance that rolls over.
- Most people only plan a daily routine (what time to sleep, what time to wake) and skip the annual and quarterly layers.

#### The 168-hour budget (the core theory)

The key theory is 168: the number of hours in a week.

The usual mistake: for the week, give to work, then with the remaining time, see whether there's time to meet parents or friends. That's not how it should be looked at. Instead, subtract in this order. The things that cannot be outsourced go first.

| Step | Line item | Example hours | Remaining | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sleep** | ~40 | | The things that cannot be outsourced: first is sleep |
| 2 | **Health & grooming** | ~8 | **118** | Gym, haircut, therapy; this cannot be outsourced (48 rounded to 50) |
| 3 | **God / faith** | 6 | | Church weekly + care group alternate weeks |
| 4 | **Spouse** | 8 | | Date night (e.g. Saturday 7–11pm) + Sunday "partnership progression" |
| 5 | **Family** | 8 | | e.g. Saturday and Sunday dinners, 4h each |
| 6 | **Social** | 6 | **90** | Tune it from four to eight; every week with a mentor or someone else |
| 7 | **Commute** | 10 | | Shrink it by where you live and how you travel |
| 8 | **Admin** | 10 | | |
| 9 | **Personal development** | 10 | **60** | Reading etc. |
| 10 | **Work** | 60 | 0 | People usually go for work forty hours; this still leaves sixty |

The key move is that the non-negotiables go in *first*. Many people say they have no time for these, but actually, you put all these aside first. Even after protecting all of it, work still gets more than a standard job.

- **Meals aren't a line item.** Eat with people, and write the time off against work or relationships, so it's always written off.
- **Recreation is baked into the protected lines.** Social is specifically the card-sending, birthday party, wedding kind of obligation.

#### Work isn't one bucket

Inside the work hours, run named budget lines:

- **Deals:** if the core skill set and main job is sales, call it deals. How many hours are spent bringing a deal in?
- **Business development / partnership development:** it's not sales.
- **System building:** developing system frameworks, and operations.
- **People management / people development:** meeting partners and key men.
- **Ideation and research.**
- **Networking**, kept separate from BD. Business development is targeted. Networking is going out there to do the hello-hello, the "introduce me" kind.

Then look at how to allocate in different business seasons. One meeting can be designed to count across several lines: it covers social, it covers business, it covers the building, everything.

#### Map reality first, then budget against goals

Take three weeks to understand, map it out, then ask: what is the goal? Which are the areas that need hours allocated? Then, based on the quarterly or monthly goal, table out the 168.

Track the 168 every week. AI can help review it; before, it was self-analysis, and the habit of tracking was needed. At the extreme, some people track in 15-minute and even 5-minute blocks and can tell you exactly what they did on any day months ago.

#### Seasons of war

Allocation is seasonal. In a season of war, family and friends are cut: that's an extra fourteen hours a week. Then later they are repaid. Tell the parents: there will be no gathering or meeting for the next three months; I'm going to fight.

A legitimate war season has five parts: name it, name what is being cut, set an end date, tell the people affected, and plan the repayment.

#### Time boxing, colour-coded calendar, weekly cadence (the execution layer)

- **Time boxing, not loose time blocking.** The key way to do it is time boxing, practised for years.
- **Example daily boxes:** wake at 7, commute, 8–9:30 morning stand-up, 9:30–11, 11–12:30, 12:30–2 lunch, 2–4 and 4–6 "mega blocks", 6:30–7 commute, 7–9 dinner, 9–10:30 evening block, power down at 11.
- **Colour per business.** The calendar has multiple colours; each colour is one business (e.g. health is green).
- **Cadence.** Each business gets **two fixed blocks a week**: one to meet partners, one to meet key men. Each weekday maps to an office. Twelve to two is booked for lunch; two to four is for heavier discussion and longer topics.
- **Feed people.** Open up all the lunch blocks and dinner blocks, then go feed people. It is very effective.
- **Flow comes after mastery.** After a decade of time boxing, it can be dropped to go with the flow, depending on priority (for example when following international timing makes boxing impossible). Flow is earned after years of boxing.

#### How it connects

- **The 168 is a relationship framework in disguise.** About 28 of the protected hours go to God, spouse, family and friends before work gets any. Time is where Faith and Relationships actually get paid.
- **Sleep and health come first because they produce capacity.** Under-allocating sleep quietly taxes every other pillar.
- **The work lines are how Time feeds Opportunities and Development.** Deals and BD hours become pipeline. Personal development hours become skill. If the lines can't be seen, it can't be told which pillar is being starved.
- **Seasons allow risk without losing trust.** A war season that is announced and repaid protects relationship capital. A silent disappearance spends it.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Non-negotiables (sleep, health, people, reflection) are scheduled before work | "No time" for health, family or reflection |
| Roughly known where last week's 168 went | Days blur together; last Tuesday can't be reconstructed |
| Work hours are split into named lines, and the starved one is visible | "Work" is one undifferentiated blob that eats everything |
| Crunches are named, time-boxed seasons with announced cuts and repayment | Permanent crunch; people find out you vanished by noticing |

### Questions to ask

- Where did your 168 actually go last week, line by line?
- What did you want time for but didn't get, and which line stole it?
- Are you in a season of war right now? Have you told the people paying for it, and when do you repay them?
- Which of your work lines is producing outputs, and which is just busy?
- What known date is going to ambush you this quarter?

## 4 · Health & Longevity

**This pillar is** the body and mind that everything else runs on: spiritual, mental and physical wellness. It sits right next to Time, with the energy levels described there.

**What it gives:** **Capacity.** The energy, clarity and resilience needed to turn allocated hours into real work. The same work hours produce very different outputs depending on sleep and energy. In AIOO terms, health is an input that changes what identical actions produce.

### How it works

#### Confess the current state, without cosmetics

Give the system the unflattering facts: conditions (e.g. gout, high blood pressure), injuries, weight gained (e.g. fourteen kg in the past three years). Give it the history (used to be a sports person, rowed for the dragon boat team in school, used to swim) and the present (now it's zero activity). This is the life-reset honesty rule applied to the body.

#### Instrument, then track

The fix is to integrate the wearable and the weight scale, then track. For food, just take a picture; it can estimate the calories. Build this and integrate it so it keeps feeding this pillar.

#### Declarations as an engineering spec

- "I am extremely healthy, all my major organs and limbs are strong and fully functional."
- "I'm a very good swimmer. I can swim fast and far with much ease. My body is in great shape."
- "I have great eyesight; I don't require visual aid."
- "I have a beautiful and amazing smile, and my teeth alignment is perfect."
- "I'm free from addiction." "Free from infectious disease." "Skin condition is flawless."

The gaps turn straight into fixes. With health, you actually know how to fix it: just do the biohack program, do the teeth, do the eyes. Everything can be done.

#### Know your own thresholds, and listen when they're crossed

Know the sleep numbers precisely. For example: survival at 35–39 hours a week, and a migraine and loss of function if under 8 hours across two days. Listen when told not to be bashing the body. After three nights of full sleep, the mind functions with a different clarity. That's how normal people feel.

#### Health is a protected budget line

About 8 hours a week for gym, grooming and therapy, allocated before work. Or going for therapy, or going to the gym: this cannot be outsourced.

#### How it connects

- **Capacity multiplies every hour downstream.** "A different clarity" is a capacity measurement.
- **Most health gaps are logistics, not willpower.** "Everything can be done" means the fix list (an eye procedure, teeth alignment, a programme) is purchasable and schedulable. That ties Health back to Finance and Time.
- **Therapy sits under health and grooming,** so mental health counts as maintenance, not as a crisis service.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Enough energy to do the work and enjoy life | Running on caffeine; skipping sleep to get more done |
| Sleep is protected and the threshold is known | Sleep debt is only noticed when sick |
| Health gaps have a dated fix list | Same health intention restated every New Year |
| Some tracking exists (weight, sleep, activity) | No data; "I feel fine" |

### Questions to ask

- How many hours do you really sleep per week, and what's your functional threshold?
- What's the honest current state you'd give an AI if you couldn't "frame or reframe" it?
- Which of your health gaps is just an unbooked appointment?
- What did your best-rested week feel like, and what produced it?

## 5 · Personal Development

**This pillar is** personal development and the capability stack: understanding one's own education journey, what certifications one has, what skills one has done. It's not career, it's personal development, and knowing your capabilities.

**What it gives:** **Skill.** The capability that Relationships and Career later convert into access and credibility. It sits before Relationships because a person needs something to offer before their network can do anything with it. It is the bridge from the inner pillars (about you) to the outer pillars (what others see and trade with you).

### How it works

#### Gifting → Stacking → Vertical (the skill development ladder)

**Step 1: find the gifting.** You must find something that you do faster than others, easier than others. People struggle, but you find it easy. This is your gifting. Most of the time, people find their gifting very boring.

- Gifting isn't passion. People want to find something exciting or passionate, and walk away from what they're naturally fast at.
- Example: someone didn't want to do banking, but that was actually his gifting; he came back to it, and there's food on the table.
- Gifting is gifting. Follow the gifting, and the required effort for result is cut down. Talent is another thing.

**Step 2: stack upstream and downstream.** After the gifting, do stacking. Find the upstream or downstream that the work impacts. For example, in sales: upstream is marketing, higher stream is branding and lead gen; downstream is operations and product. A salesman has to go find leads and do customer service, so it stacks.

**Step 3: go vertical.** If you're in sales in this place, you can do sales in another place. The stacked skill set carries across industries. That's how more skill sets are developed.

#### The capability inventory (what the pillar holds)

- Education journey and certifications.
- Skills done, mastery level, projects done.
- Exposure turned into learning: there was this job, this exposure was given, and this is what was learned. Then it is indexed.
- "What skill should I pick up next?", answered by the system: mastery level, projects done, then build the understanding.
- Platforms that have skill assessment and recommend courses.
- A learning base: whatever books are read, take a PDF copy, put it in, then write a review. Any video watched is archived there.
- Auto-tagging so one experience lands in skill set, career and branding together.

#### Protected time

About 10 hours a week of personal development (reading and so on) in the 168.

#### The five abilities (development order)

When sizing people up, assess in this order:
1. **Learning ability:** how fast? What is the person's medium and mode of learning?
2. **Execution.**
3. **Communication.** Many people are great at learning and execution, yet their roles are small and their impact small, because they can't communicate.
4. **Leadership:** can they bring people together? Is their 人情世故 (social judgment) good?
5. **Management:** totally different things.

#### How it connects

- **Gifting is an efficiency argument.** It isn't "follow your gift because it's fulfilling". It lowers the effort per unit of result: the same action costs less input.
- **Stacking keeps skills compounding on one value chain** instead of scattering. Each new skill raises the value of the existing ones because they're adjacent. Random courses don't compound.
- **The five abilities are a roadmap.** Each one unlocks bigger positions in Career.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Skills compound along one chain toward the vision | Random courses; no clear next skill |
| The gifting can be named and is backed by evidence | Chasing "passion" while discounting what you're fast at |
| There is a written answer to "what skill next, and why" | Skills going stale; learning only when forced |
| Exposures are turned into recorded learnings | Experiences happen and evaporate |

### Questions to ask

- What do people always ask you for help with, that you find easy and maybe boring?
- What's directly upstream and downstream of that, and which one would change the most if you had it?
- Of the five abilities, which is your current ceiling?
- What did your last big exposure teach you, and is it written down anywhere?

## 6 · Relationships

**This pillar is** family, people and relationship: family, spouse, friends, mentors, clients and the wider network, held in a Rolodex.

**What it gives:** **Access.** Entry to rooms, people, opportunities and help that can't be reached alone. In the Four E's, access is literally where you can bring people into. Relationships give the room; Career then gives something solid to say once inside it.

### How it works

#### FORM + Health + Dreams: what to know about each person

- **F**amily
- **O**ccupation
- **R**ecreation (relationships fall under this too)
- **M**oney (finances)
- **Health**
- **Dreams and desires** — the most important

The more you know about a person's desire, the closer you become. Some people you've known a long time, but you don't know their family, their passion, their hobby. Some people you've known three weeks only, and you know a lot. It's how fast and how willing and how much you trust each other to exchange this information.

The practice: each time you meet and catch up with somebody, note the interesting information. After meeting the person, give a quick review: met this person, there are some key updates (family, and so on).

#### Deal cards (BANT): one person can carry many opportunities

Set up a separate card called a deal card. For one relationship, you can have many deal cards. Each card holds the context and the opportunity, then the four key factors:

- **Budget**
- **Authority:** who can make the decision?
- **Needs**
- **Timeline**

It is universal, not just sales. For example, recruiting a person into the ecosystem: what's the budget? How much salary per month? CRM behaviours sit around the card: when you last followed up, the next steps, important dates (date of birth, business anniversaries), and catch-up frequency.

#### The Rolodex bootstrap: the wedding and funeral test

Circles are classified, and redefined as you age. For relationship onboarding, the first thing to start with is your wedding: who will come, or who did you invite? Next, your funeral: who do you think will show up? Then you roughly know this is your circle.

Then map everyone: secondary school friends, hobby friends, family members, all of them in a contact list. Primary school friends, secondary school friends, write them all out, then start targeting for referrals. You build a Rolodex first.

#### Tier by frequency, tag by type, budget each relationship

- **Frequency tiers:** weekly (people seen or communicated with weekly, and seen at least within a month), monthly, about four times a year (birthdays), and every few years. Categorise by the different circles.
- **Meta-tags:** by hobby, client, whatever.
- **Allocation:** how much time per person, and what the budget is to maintain this relationship. Keep an internal figure for how much will be invested in certain relationships. When it gets too many, drop some and then cycle.
- **Archive tier:** a once-a-year revisit, with the AI nudging: it has been nine months since you chatted with this person, please make an appointment, and propose which day is best.

#### The service cadence: touch everyone four times a year

Service clients four times a year: Q1 is CNY, Q2 is the anniversary, Q3 is Hari Raya / Deepavali, Q4 is Christmas. Touch base with every one.

- The budget is gifts twice and cards twice.
- The Q2 anniversary note: thank you, we're still in business, thank you for supporting us.
- This is the Serve X framework for business, and it can be used for personal relationships too.
- The rule: **broadcast by season, follow up by context.** With this person, how often is follow-up needed? There's a time and budget allocation.
- The AI can host events, handle RSVPs, and propose venue and food against the guest list and budget.

#### Protected hours in the 168

Spouse 8, family 8, social 6, plus God 6, all before work. Open lunch and dinner blocks to go feed people. Keep a standing weekly hangout with a mentor.

#### Mentorship and access

- If nobody brings you in, you're dead. If someone brings you in but doesn't take care of you, doesn't protect you, doesn't give you an agenda, you're also dead.
- The first meeting, you rely on someone's introduction to get in. At the second meeting, when your substance is tested, you're dead. So for every second meeting, bring a "deity" (神灵): a heavyweight who supplies credibility you don't yet have.
- Access compounds over decades and starts with low-status service: running errands for someone who only meets a handful of people a year, until you are one of them.
- A mentor is needed to see the invisible rewards. The mentor guides you and helps you understand whether these things are right, whether they're worth it.

#### How it connects

- **Closeness comes from information exchanged, not from time.** Trust can be deliberately sped up by exchanging FORM + Health + Dreams, starting with your own.
- **Person cards and deal cards are kept separate on purpose.** A relationship isn't a deal. One person can hold many opportunities over decades; keeping them apart stops friends being treated as pipeline while still capturing opportunity when it exists. This is the handoff to Opportunities: the Rolodex is tied to work associates, so "who should I do it with?" becomes answerable.
- **Maintaining relationships is a budget, not a feeling.** Time per person, money per person, touches per year, and conscious pruning. Treated as a portfolio, nobody important falls off silently.
- **Relationships feed Time and Health too.** Meals, church and gym routed through relationships let one hour serve several pillars.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Key relationships are maintained on purpose, on a cadence | Reaching out only when something is needed |
| The dreams of the closest people are known | Birthdays forgotten; can't name a close friend's current goal |
| At least one real mentor, with a relationship that grows | No one who would vouch for you in a closed room |
| Opportunities are captured as deal cards linked to people | Opportunities live in the head and die there |
| Pruning and cycling are conscious | The list just grows and everyone gets neglected equally |

### Questions to ask

- Who would be at your funeral? Who did you leave off the wedding list who should be on it?
- For your five closest people, can you name each one's dream right now?
- Who haven't you spoken to in nine months who matters?
- Who is bringing you into rooms, and are you being the kind of person they want to protect?
- Are you treating any friend as a deal, or missing a deal because you didn't write it down?

## 7 · Career & Track Record

**This pillar is** professional and career architecture: a person's professional history and the evidence of what they've done, maintained as a living file.

**What it gives:** **Credibility.** Proof others can check. Career gives credibility, which Brand then amplifies into opportunity. Credibility is evidence, not effort: undocumented skill is worth far less to everything downstream. It is the substance needed for the second meeting.

### How it works

#### The living evidence file

- **LinkedIn** as the public layer.
- **A cover letter:** how do you introduce yourself professionally?
- **References and reference checks, collected proactively:** go and get people for reference letters, reference checks, testimonials, everything you get from people.
- **A track record, filed and indexed:** whatever track record you have, file it, index it. Always update the cover letter, always update the resume.
- **Private and fuller than any public profile.** Everything a person has cannot be put on LinkedIn. This file sorts all professional involvement and scope across multiple roles, and it helps manage positioning as a professional.
- **Architecture questions** the system should then answer: what should you take next? Where should you apply? Is this job offer good or not? How should you be compensated? Architect your career.
- **Capture at the moment of doing:** each time you do something, give a reflection, give some information, send the supporting material, and sort it out. Evidence decays if it isn't captured; a testimonial is easiest to get on the day of the win.

#### Position cards: every role is priced by six fields

Position cards: you can have multiple roles, multiple cards. Each card has:

- **Title**
- **Role**
- **Responsibility**
- **Risk.** Risk explains pay gaps. "You're as smart as me, you work the same, why do I get more? Because I have risk." First, reputational risk. Second, financial risk. If the company collapses, a lot of people have no implication; it's the owner's name that stinks.
- **Input.** Input is more than hours. Many people only put in time and expertise. Some people put in money. Some put in their life (放命). Look past the surface at skin in the game.
- **Rewards.** Rewards include the invisible. Sometimes the opportunity itself is a reward. If you compare money, you'll definitely cry; people will think you're an idiot for still taking this job. But they don't get it.

#### The five abilities decide how big the position gets

Learning, execution, communication, leadership, management. These are the five core things that determine how far a position goes.

#### The first 90 days, and "taking another card"

- From perception: that's why someone is hired. After hiring, the first 90 days is to find out. Then confirm them, change them, or add on to them.
- The people given more opportunity are people who, after their day job, take on another card.

#### Experience and evidence (Four E's)

Experience means areas worked in for years, where you were responsible. Evidence is your track record: evidence that you are successful in certain things. Exposure, where you weren't responsible for results, counts for less.

#### How it connects

- **Position cards change how a career is seen.** A person isn't one job title but a portfolio of roles, each with its own risk and reward profile. That makes it possible to justify a lower-paid card for a bigger invisible reward, and to know when unpaid risk is being carried.
- **Career sits between Relationships and Brand.** Relationships give the room (access). Career gives something solid to say once in it. Brand then spreads that to people who weren't in the room.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Wins recorded at the time, with proof and a named voucher | Achievements forgotten; CV updated only when job-hunting |
| Capability can be shown, not just told | Credibility depends on people taking your word |
| Position cards are held, and each one's risk and reward is known | One undifferentiated "job" and a salary comparison |
| Career moves are chosen against the north star | Moves happen by default or by whoever offers first |

### Questions to ask

- What's the best piece of work you did this year, and who could vouch for it in writing?
- What position cards do you hold, and which carries risk you're not being rewarded for?
- What invisible reward are you getting that a salary comparison misses?
- Of your Four E's, is your Evidence keeping up with your Exposure?
- What extra card are you holding beyond the day job?

## 8 · Personal Brand & Authority

**This pillar is** visibility: what the public knows about a person, through what medium, with what content. What do we put out there for people to know us?

**What it gives:** **Opportunity.** Branding gives business opportunity. Career makes a person credible to people who've worked with them; Brand makes them credible to people who haven't, so opportunities come to them instead of them chasing opportunities. Brand only amplifies what exists upstream, so it sits after Skill and Career.

### How it works

#### The three questions of the pillar

1. What does the public know about us?
2. Through what medium?
3. With what content?

#### Brand is managed perception

Perception is how people form the perception about you. It is built from the **Four E's** (Education, Exposure, Experience, Evidence) plus **Association, Access, Resources**. People introduce themselves by those anchors: their school, club, team or company. This is perception management. It can be changed: once a higher perception can be displayed, a person can keep remodelling.

#### Perception determines position, which determines opportunity

Based on perception, people give you a position. Positioning and perception are the key. Why do people miss opportunities? People start from the project, but they don't understand why they're not that guy. Why does someone else have more opportunity? The opportunity problem is usually a perception problem, not a reason to work harder on projects.

#### Experiences feed the brand automatically

An exposure (e.g. an overseas trip) gets written up and is then picked up by skill set and by personal branding. Going on stage is tagged as a branding opportunity.

#### A declared authority

Brand has a declaration like every other pillar. Examples: "I'm a renowned business authority worldwide. I'm a successful and influential business figure." "I'm an author and have multiple best-selling books… the creator of life-changing business systems and training programs."

#### How it connects

- **Substance first, visibility second.** Without Evidence and Skill, branding is noise or, worse, a credibility gap that the second meeting exposes.
- **Association is a brand lever that can be borrowed.** Perception includes whose child you are and who you know. Being visibly associated with credible people and institutions is part of the brand, which links this pillar back to Relationships.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| The right people know what you're great at, so opportunities come to you | Invisible outside a small circle |
| Public perception matches the identity being built | Known for something outgrown, or for the wrong thing |
| Content is backed by evidence | Visibility without substance; fails the second meeting |
| What you want to be known for can be said in one line | No positioning; you're whatever the last person assumed |

### Questions to ask

- What do you want to be known for, in one line?
- If someone searched for you today, what would they find, and would it get you the position you want?
- Which of your Four E's is invisible to the public right now?
- Why are you not "that guy" yet for the opportunity you want? What would change the perception?

## 9 · Work, Business & Opportunities

**This pillar is** work, business and opportunities: your work, your workstation. What's your job? Do you have any opportunity? Do you have any business? If there are projects, there's a project file. Only work-related.

**What it gives:** **Finance.** This is where effort is converted into money: jobs, projects, deals, ventures. Opportunity becomes finance. Deals closed are outputs; financial freedom (the declared net worth and passive income) is the outcome.

### How it works

#### A file per business, project and opportunity

Anything that's a project going to business gets a business file, which ideally becomes a vault that can be shared. Exposures go here too and are cross-filed into skills and branding.

#### A one-sentence deal filter based on the current constraint

Write the rule from the actual situation. Example, from a constraint of high paper valuation but no liquidity yet: take cash-flowing opportunities. The next deal forward should have cash-first commitment, and then after that, equity upside. Should not take pure equity deals.

The system can also say which businesses to strengthen and which to cut. A written filter, enforced with real context, protects against saying yes to everything.

#### The four-question opportunity gate

For every incoming opportunity, answer:

1. **Should I take it or not?**
2. **If I take it, what is the structure?**
3. **What is the compensation?**
4. **Who should I do it with?** This is answerable because the Rolodex is tied to a lot of work associates.

Once this exists, the system says which ones can be taken and which can't, what the deal is, how much, then who to do it with.

#### Context is non-negotiable

Keep giving context. Otherwise you're listening to a mad AI, trained by two million random people. The filter only works with real context.

#### Deal cards and BANT

Deal cards (from Relationships) are the record format for each opportunity: Budget, Authority, Needs, Timeline, plus last follow-up and next step.

#### Work hours are budgeted by type

Deals, BD/partnerships, system building, people development, ideation/research, networking, re-weighted in different business seasons.

#### AIOO for planning work

After position comes plan. Reverse-engineer the plan: what is the goal? What is the action? What input do you need to put in? What is the output? How do you get the outcome? After you get the result, re-edit, change, try. Don't copy other people's actions blindly: you never do it 100%, and your inputs differ.

#### Position-card logic applies to deals

Price risk and input, not just hours. Value the invisible rewards.

#### How it connects

- **The deal filter sits downstream of Finance as well as upstream of it.** A cash-first rule only exists because the finance pillar showed a liquidity gap. A later pillar sends a constraint back up the flywheel.
- **"Who should I do it with" connects Relationships and Opportunities.** Opportunity quality depends on the people in it.
- **Outputs are not the outcome.** A busy pipeline can feel productive while something is missing.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| A written rule decides which opportunities are taken | Chasing everything, or nothing in the pipeline |
| Each opportunity has a card: BANT, last follow-up, next step | Opportunities discussed once and forgotten |
| The outcome the outputs are building toward is known | Busy with outputs, but the outcome isn't moving |
| Partners are chosen deliberately | Doing deals with whoever is nearby |

### Questions to ask

- What are you working on right now, and what outcome is each piece building toward?
- What kind of opportunity do you say no to, in one sentence?
- For your biggest current opportunity, can you state Budget, Authority, Need and Timeline?
- Are you copying someone's actions without their inputs?

## 10 · Finance & Wealth

**This pillar is** money in, money out, and what it's building: net worth composition and how to grow it; cash flow and the cash flow statement; expenses; goals; investment (are you too aggressive?) and an updated investment portfolio.

**What it gives:** **Lifestyle.** Finance becomes lifestyle. It also sends constraints back upstream: the base number and liquidity decide what risks, seasons and deals the earlier pillars can afford.

### How it works

#### The four tiers: reverse-engineering the survival number

1. **Basic necessities:** the minimum to keep the lights on, things moving.
2. **Commitments:** the house, a mortgage, insurance.
3. **Lifestyle:** entertainment, holidays. Those are budget.
4. **Investment.**

#### Why: the base number decides how much risk and load someone can carry

Assess how much money someone needs. If you're going to fight together, you need to know how much is needed as a base. Reverse-engineer how much result needs to be created. Then it's known how much stress and how much load a person can take; their finances can all be planned out. Every person should do the exercise.

Applied to leading people: know everyone's finances, everyone's family, their load. A target can't fairly be set that someone's finances can't survive.

#### Cash flow statement plus net worth

Track expenses, cash flow, net worth composition, and an investment portfolio checked for over-aggression.

#### Paper wealth vs liquidity

A high valuation with no liquidity yet is not spendable wealth. Declare net worth and liquid assets as separate lines so valuation is never mistaken for wealth. The liquidity gap is what produces a cash-first deal rule.

#### Tools are now cheap

Planning, budgeting, receipts: photograph them and it's automatic. Before AI, such an app would have cost hundreds of thousands to build and thousands a month to maintain.

#### Declared financial end-states

The north star declares net worth, liquid assets, passive income and monthly/annual earnings targets, each separately.

#### How it connects

- **The base number connects finance to courage.** Knowing the floor (tiers 1 + 2) tells how long a season of war, a low-paid card with big invisible rewards, or an equity bet can be afforded. Not knowing it makes every risk feel either reckless or terrifying.
- **Finance sends constraints back upstream.** Liquidity shapes the opportunity filter. The survival number shapes how hard the 168 can be pushed.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| The base number and monthly cash flow are known | No idea where money goes |
| Money is visibly building toward declared goals | Income arrives and disappears |
| Risk-taking is sized against the floor | Taking risks without knowing the floor, or avoiding all risk out of vague fear |
| Paper and liquid wealth are tracked separately | Valuation treated as spendable |

### Questions to ask

- What's the minimum you need each month (necessities + commitments)?
- How many months could you survive a season of war on what you have?
- What's your money building toward, and is that written as a declaration?
- How much of your "wealth" is liquid?

## 11 · Lifestyle & Experiences

**This pillar is** lifestyle, recreation and material: what a person wants to enjoy — experiences, things, places, how they live. You have a goal.

**What it gives:** **Legacy.** Finance becomes lifestyle, and lifestyle sits just before legacy because how a person lives, and especially who they share it with, is part of what they leave behind. Lifestyle declarations are largely about hosting and sharing: a yacht to host loved ones and friends, annual family trips, parents retiring without stress.

### How it works

#### Every lifestyle goal gets a budget

Holidays and the rest are budget. After the budget is set, what are the lifestyle goals? Holiday, everything. Lifestyle is tier 3 of the finance tiers.

#### Buy on trajectory: the system says *when*

You want to buy a watch, whatever. Look at what you want, and the system tells you: buy when you can have it, based on the trajectory. If you want it sooner, you need to take the extra hours to go do some stuff to earn it. A lifestyle goal isn't a yes or no; it's a "when", tied to the financial trajectory.

#### Goals and dreams map onto the pillars

Lifestyle goals are written as declarations like every other pillar. Examples:
- "I'm a real estate mogul… I live in a beautiful and luxurious house."
- "I have every car that I like, and I can afford to change them as I please."
- "I have an estate of all my favourite timepieces", possibly run as a hobby club (a horology club).
- "I own a yacht, and I can use it to host my loved ones and friends for parties."
- "We have annual trips."

#### Recreation is built into the 168

Recreation is baked in, inside the social, family and spouse hours, not left to chance.

#### How it connects

- **Lifestyle is earned and planned, not used to escape.** Tying each goal to a budget and a trajectory removes the guilt (it's planned) and the overspending (it waits for the trajectory).
- **Lifestyle goals send motivation back upstream.** "Take the extra hours" turns a desire into an allocation question (Time) and an opportunity question (Opportunities).

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| Enjoyment is planned, budgeted and earned | All work, nothing to look forward to |
| Lifestyle goals have a "when" tied to trajectory | Spending ahead of what finances support |
| Experiences are shared with people who matter | Lifestyle used to escape or to impress |

### Questions to ask

- What are you looking forward to in the next 90 days?
- What would you buy or do now if you could, and when does your trajectory say you can?
- Which of your lifestyle goals would also strengthen a relationship?
- Are you enjoying anything, or postponing all of it to "after"?

## 12 · Legacy & Stewardship

**This pillar is** legacy, stewardship and succession: what will you leave behind when you die? What will pass down? *Legacy* is what is left, *stewardship* is how what has been given is managed in the meantime, and *succession* is who carries it on.

**What it gives:** **back to Faith.** Legacy tells you what the outcome is. The outcome you're fighting for is your life vision: what will the outcome become? This is the summary, and then you go back to your faith. It is the test of the entire flywheel: if the projected outcome doesn't match the life vision, something upstream is off, usually Faith (wrong direction) or Identity (unclear vision). That's why the loop returns to Faith instead of ending.

### How it works

#### Legacy is the projected outcome

Once the other pillars are in place, the system tells you what the outcome is. Compare that projection against the life vision (pillar 2). In AIOO terms, Legacy is the Outcome-level view of a whole life.

#### Legacy declarations

Legacy is declared like every other pillar, and is mostly about other people's lives. Examples:
- **Family:** "I'm a filial and loving son, who has helped my parents fulfil their dreams." "Our parents are able to retire and maintain their lifestyle happily with no stress or strain. I have helped my siblings achieve massive success." "A loving, caring, responsible father to our children."
- **Ministry:** "I serve full time in ministry… a server and leader in my church care group… I go on mission trips to help people in need and spread the love of God. I guided and led at least 1,000 people into the house of God."
- **Philanthropy:** "I'm a kind and generous philanthropist. I started a foundation to help the needy… I sponsor orphans… I sponsor abandoned old folks."
- **Work that outlives you:** "I'm an author and have multiple best-selling books… the creator of life-changing business systems and training programs."

#### Passing on frameworks

Creating frameworks and training is a way of passing things down; if it stops, many people forget. Write the frameworks down: framework is very important, and without context, it's probably nothing.

#### Mentorship as a chain

A mentor walks with a mentee for years; the mentee goes on to mentor and coach others, as a group and as a company. The mentor-to-mentee chain is a concrete legacy mechanism.

#### How it connects

- **Legacy is outward-facing** in a way the earlier pillars aren't: parents retiring, siblings succeeding, orphans, people brought to church, people trained.
- **Every turn of the flywheel starts deeper.** Thinking about what is left behind changes what a person believes their life is for, and the next turn runs on that sharper direction.

### Healthy vs stalled

| Healthy | Stalled |
|---|---|
| What is to be left is known, and today's choices point that way | Never considered; living quarter to quarter |
| Some giving, mentoring or teaching already exists | "Later, when I've made it" |
| The projected outcome is regularly compared against the vision | Vision and trajectory never compared |

### Questions to ask

- What do you want people to say about you at your funeral?
- Who are you building this for?
- If your current trajectory continues for 20 years, what's the outcome, and does it match your life vision?
- What are you already passing down, and to whom?

## How the pillars interlock

### Diagnose upstream

Because each pillar runs on the currency of the one before it, a problem that shows up in one pillar is usually caused one or two pillars earlier.

| Symptom shows up in… | Points upstream to… |
|---|---|
| **Opportunities:** "why does he get the opportunities and not me?" | **Perception and position** (Identity / Brand / Career). People start from the project but don't understand why they're not that guy |
| **Execution / action:** people don't act, or act without results | **Purpose and identity** (Faith / Identity). Why can't people do it? Because they have no purpose; understand what our identity is |
| **Same actions, different results** | **Inputs:** resources, family, context, network (Relationships, Finance, History in Identity) |
| **Clarity and functioning at work** | **Health / sleep:** three nights of real sleep give a different clarity |
| **"No time" for family, health, church** | **Allocation** (Time): the non-negotiables weren't reserved first. Put all these aside first |
| **Health gaps** | **Identity** declarations turned into fix lists: everything can be done |
| **Which deals to take** | **Finance** feeds back: no liquidity leads to a cash-first rule |
| **How hard someone can be pushed at work** | **Finance** base number: how much stress, how much load they can take |
| **Second meetings that fail** | **Substance** (Development / Career), or a borrowed "deity" from Relationships |
| **A messy AI life system** | **Framework before context:** first turn it into the framework, then put the life story in |

### Backward constraints

The chain flows forward (faith → … → legacy), but several constraints travel backward:
- **Finance → Opportunities:** liquidity shapes the deal filter.
- **Finance → Time:** the base number decides how long a war season can last.
- **Lifestyle → Time:** you need to take the extra hours to afford what you want.
- **Legacy → Faith:** the projected outcome sends you back to belief.
- **Relationships ↔ Time:** relationships are reserved first in the 168, but in war seasons they're the line that gets cut and later repaid.

### One hour, many pillars

Design single activities that pay several pillars at once:
- Meals are always with someone: work + relationships.
- One meeting covers social, business, the building, everything.
- One logged exposure is tagged into skill set, career and branding.
- Church covers faith + relationships (God hours plus care group).

### Where to start

Start governing whichever area most needs a fix, then slowly move to the other areas, because it's a flywheel. Onboarding order: instruments (BaZi, Enneagram) → vision board / north star → current situation → pillar by pillar, wherever you feel you can start.
